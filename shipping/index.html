The honest math

We don't hold stock. Your piece doesn't exist until you order it — a printer in the US makes it, then hands it to the carrier. That trade gets you designs too specific to warehouse, and it costs a few days. Here is exactly where they go.

Timing

Printing2–5 business days
Transit, US3–4 business days
Order to door5–9 business days

Most orders land in about a week. If you need it for a specific date, order nine business days ahead and you're covered.

Where it's made

Partner print facilities in the US, usually North Carolina or California, whichever is closer to you. The label says DEAD FUTURES. One honesty note: colorway names are ours; the blanks underneath are standard catalog garments, named in the spec sheet on every product page.

If something's wrong

Misprint, damage, wrong item: send a photo within 30 days of delivery and a replacement goes to the printer that week, free. Wrong size is the one we can't fix — each piece is printed for you, so there is no shelf for it to go back on. Check the size chart before you order — it's measured flat, in centimeters and inches.
