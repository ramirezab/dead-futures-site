The futures we didn't get

Every era draws the future it deserves, and almost none of them arrive. The 40s were promised chrome zeppelins and engines with names. The 80s were warned about neon rain and rented bodies. Somewhere ahead of us is the one that grows back. They all missed the exit — but the drawings survived.

DEAD FUTURES prints those drawings. Three timelines, 53 designs, made one at a time when you order — because stockpiling merchandise for futures that never happened felt like a joke we didn't want to explain to a warehouse.

  • TL-2077 Cyberpunk — The future the 80s were afraid of.
  • TL-1949 Dieselpunk — The future the 40s were promised.
  • TL-2109 Biopunk — The future that grows back.

One-person shop. The shipping page has the honest math on what print-on-demand costs you in days.
