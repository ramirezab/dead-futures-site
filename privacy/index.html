Document PV-1 · privacy

What we keep

A one-person shop selling printed pieces has no use for a profile of you. This is the whole of it.

What we collect

  • — To fulfill an order: your name, shipping address and email. Nothing else is asked for and nothing else is stored.
  • — Your cart lives in your own browser's local storage, on your device. It is never sent to us until you check out, and clearing your browser data erases it.

What we never see

Card numbers. Payment is not live in this build; when it opens it runs through PayPal, on PayPal's own page, so your card details go to them and never touch this site or any server of ours. We are told only whether the payment succeeded.

Who it is shared with

  • — Printful, the printer, receives the name and address so they can make and send the parcel.
  • — PayPal, to take the payment.
  • — The carrier, to deliver it.

That is the entire list. Your details are not sold, rented, traded or used to advertise to you elsewhere.

Tracking

No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, no cookie banner — because there is nothing to consent to. The site sets no cookies of its own.

How long, and your say over it

Order records are kept for three years, which is the general IRS record-keeping period, then deleted. You can ask for a copy of what we hold on you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be erased once the order is delivered and the return window has closed. Write to info.deadfutures@gmail.com and expect an answer within 30 days.

See also the terms and returns.
